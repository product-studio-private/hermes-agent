# daily-brief — Pocket's morning brief format

Use this skill whenever the owner asks for "el brief", "el briefing", "la
mañana", or when the morning-brief cron job fires.

## Format

1. **Pendientes de hoy** — everything stored with today's date or earlier,
   oldest deadline first. One line each: what + when.
2. **Urgente/sensible a tiempo** — anything with a hard cutoff (a payment
   window, an appointment, a deadline). One line each.
3. **Lo más importante hoy** — exactly one line: the single thing you'd
   push them to do first.

## Rules

- Under 10 lines total. No greeting preamble beyond "Buenos días" or
  "Listo, tu brief de hoy:".
- No inventing pendings. If memory is empty, say so plainly and ask them
  to send today's list.
- Dates and times in the owner's timezone (America/Bogota default).
